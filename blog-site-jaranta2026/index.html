About Digital Interface

Digital Interface was started by a group of college buddies whose common interest was computers. It was formally organized into a company in August of 1984.

The company has since grown to become a major player in the computer industry in Southern Philippines. Serving information technology needs, its satisfied customers include multinational companies, government, and small and medium sized businesses.

About This OJT Program

This On-the-Job Training took place at Digital Interface from June 15 to August 7, 2026, as a requirement of the College of Information Technology Education at North Eastern Mindanao State University. Over eight weeks, I rendered a total of 324 hours of training.

Based on my weekly accomplishments, Digital Interface taught me how a computer company runs day to day. In customer service, I learned to answer calls, assist walk-in customers, handle service requests, and make follow-up calls. In documentation, I learned the Repair and Field Service (RFS) process: encoding units into the monitoring system, preparing work orders, processing billing, and releasing verified units.

I also gained hands-on exposure to hardware. I observed technicians troubleshoot and repair printers, assisted with installing RAM and a power supply unit, set up newly purchased Epson printers with their drivers, tested computer monitors, and learned to tell genuine Epson inks from counterfeit ones.

My OJT Experience and Activities

This website documents my eight-week OJT journey at Digital Interface, where I gained experience in customer service, repair and field service documentation, billing, monitoring systems, and computer hardware-related activities.

Week 1

June 15–19, 2026

My first week focused on familiarizing myself with Digital Interface's workplace policies, departments, and daily workflow. I learned the Repair and Field Service (RFS) procedures, practiced responding to telephone inquiries and assisting walk-in customers, encoded newly received repair units into the monitoring system, prepared repair documents, learned the billing process, and observed the proper release of repaired units.

Week 2

June 22–26, 2026

During my second week, I began performing actual customer service duties under staff supervision. I handled customer inquiries and service requests, processed billing for repaired and defective RFS units, coordinated repair completion updates, encoded new repair units, organized documents, generated work orders, assisted with releasing verified units, updated the monitoring database, and conducted customer follow-up calls.

Week 3

June 29–July 3, 2026

I continued handling customer inquiries, repair transactions, documentation, monitoring records, and follow-up calls. I also observed technicians troubleshooting printer output-quality problems and cleaning printer printheads, giving me a better understanding of printer maintenance and the technical services provided by Digital Interface.

Week 4

July 6–10, 2026

I continued my regular customer service, billing, documentation, and repair-monitoring responsibilities. I also observed technicians replacing defective printer power supply units and printer ink system assemblies, helping me understand common printer hardware problems and the repair procedures used to address them.

Week 5

July 13–17, 2026

I maintained my regular duties involving customer assistance, RFS transactions, work orders, documentation, and repair updates. I attended an Epson product meeting about identifying genuine Epson inks and counterfeit ink products. I also helped list and organize previously disposed units to support accurate inventory records.

Week 6

July 20–24, 2026

I continued handling customer service, billing, and documentation while gaining additional exposure to desktop computer hardware. I assisted in installing RAM modules, replacing a desktop computer power supply unit (PSU), and diagnosing the possible causes of sudden computer shutdowns under technical guidance.

Week 7

July 27–31, 2026

I continued managing customer transactions, repair documentation, monitoring records, and follow-up calls. I also assisted customers in initializing newly purchased Epson printers, installed printer drivers, and checked printer functionality before the devices were used by customers.

Week 8

August 3–7, 2026

During my eighth week, I continued assisting customers, processing RFS transactions, updating repair records, and preparing work orders. I tested computer monitors, verified their display quality and hardware functionality, and ensured that units were working properly before delivery to customers.
